# Cost Optimization

Cost reduction should target the largest cost drivers first (Pareto
principle) rather than being spread evenly across all cost categories,
since uniform cuts rarely move the profitability needle on the categories
that matter. Renegotiating supplier terms is generally lower-risk than
headcount or quality reductions and should be attempted first. Cost
increases that outpace revenue growth over consecutive periods are an early
warning sign of margin compression and should trigger a cost-structure
review before profitability materially declines. Fixed costs should be
converted to variable costs where feasible (e.g., outsourcing, usage-based
vendor contracts) to improve resilience during demand downturns.
