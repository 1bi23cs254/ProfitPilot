# Inventory Optimization

Slow-moving inventory (no meaningful sales velocity over 60+ days) ties up
working capital and increases holding cost; it should be discounted,
bundled, or liquidated rather than held indefinitely. Fast-moving,
high-margin SKUs should be prioritized for safety-stock investment to avoid
stockouts, since a stockout on a high-margin product has a larger profit
impact than one on a low-margin product. ABC analysis (classifying inventory
into A/B/C tiers by revenue contribution) helps focus replenishment
attention on the ~20% of SKUs that typically drive ~80% of profit.
Just-in-time replenishment reduces holding cost but increases stockout risk
during demand spikes or supply delays, so it is best applied to predictable,
low-variance demand items only.
