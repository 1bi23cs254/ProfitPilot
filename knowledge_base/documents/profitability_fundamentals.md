# Profitability Fundamentals

Net profit margin is the clearest single indicator of overall business
health: below 5% is considered fragile for most product-based businesses,
5-15% is typical/stable, and above 15% indicates strong pricing power or
cost discipline. Revenue growth without margin improvement or stability is
not necessarily healthy growth — it can mask deteriorating unit economics.
Return on investment (ROI) should be evaluated per product line or channel,
not only at the aggregate business level, since aggregate profitability can
hide underperforming segments that a top-line view would miss. Forecasted
revenue decline combined with a declining margin trend is the strongest
combined signal that intervention is needed.
